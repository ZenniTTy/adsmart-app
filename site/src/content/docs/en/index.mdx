## What AdSmart does

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Queries" icon="magnifier">
		Ask about campaigns, spend, and results. Claude fetches data from your Google Ads account in chat.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Safe changes" icon="pencil">
		Budgets, status, bids, keywords, and ads. Every change shows a preview validated by Google before it is applied.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Undo and history" icon="document">
		Each change is stored on your computer and can be undone, also with a preview.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Local and free" icon="laptop">
		Uses your own Google Cloud service account. Nothing goes through project servers.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## How it works

<Steps>

1. **Install** the `.mcpb` file in Claude Desktop with a double-click.
2. **Set up** by asking in chat: "Me ajude a configurar a AdSmart" (the guided setup is in Portuguese). Claude walks you through Google Cloud and Google Ads with you.
3. **Chat.** Ask for reports or changes. To change anything, Claude shows a preview and applies only after you confirm.

</Steps>
